# Swrite — Novelist & Author Studio

**Swrite** is an author-first writing suite designed for novelists, pantsers, plotters, and worldbuilders.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. Smart Novel Keyboard & Manuscript Engine
- **1 Enter**: Standard manuscript paragraph with automatic first-line indentation (`1.5em`) and zero paragraph gap.
- **2 Enters**: Automatically inserts a centered Scene Break ornament (`* * *`, `❦`, or `◆ ◆ ◆`).
- **3 Enters**: Intelligent Chapter Break (prompts for new chapter title, creates new chapter node, and resets header/footer).
- **Typewriter Centering Mode**: Keeps the active writing line centered on the screen.
- **Dual View Engine**:
  - **Focus Continuous Flow**: Smooth, distraction-free drafting.
  - **Live Paginated Book Preview**: Real-time simulated 6" x 9" trade paperback pages with alternating running top headers and bottom page numbers.

### 2. The Editorial Partner & Planning Suite
- **Interactive Character Bible**: Comprehensive profile sheets with character archetypes, motivations, flaws, secrets, and dialogue voice notes.
- **Plot Beats & Timeline**: 3-Act Structure / Beat Sheet view with tension levels (1–10) and target chapter linking.
- **AI Editorial Co-Pilot (BYOK)**: Supports Google Gemini, OpenAI (GPT-4o), Anthropic Claude, and Local Offline models via Ollama. Features built-in one-click actions:
  - *Critique Active Chapter*
  - *Check Character Voice & Dialogue*
  - *Continuity & Clue Check*
  - *Brainstorm Next Scene Hook*
- **Brainstorm Scratchpad**: Freeform worldbuilding and unsorted research notes.

### 3. Highlighting & Collapsible Margin Inspector
- **Multi-color Highlights**: Critique (Red), Sensory (Amber), Fact Check (Blue), Favorite/Golden (Green), and Todo (Purple).
- **Threaded Margin Notes**: Attach editorial notes to highlighted text; filter by open/resolved.
- **Chapter Synopsis & POV**: Dedicated chapter POV character dropdown and synopsis.

### 4. Custom Theming & Literary Typography
- Curated Themes: *Midnight Studio, Vintage Parchment, Clean Manuscript (Light), Nordic Frost, Botanical Velvet, Typewriter Amber*.
- Custom Theme Builder with live color swatches.
- Bundled literary fonts: *EB Garamond, Merriweather, Lora, Playfair Display, Atkinson Hyperlegible, Inter, JetBrains Mono*, plus Local System Fonts.

### 5. Multi-Format Book Compiler
- **Print PDF**: Configurable trim sizes (6"x9", 5.5"x8.5", A5, Letter) with running headers, footers, and page numbers.
- **EPUB 3**: Formatted for Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) and e-readers.
- **DOCX**: Standard Manuscript Format for editors and literary agents.
- **Markdown**: Clean `.md` manuscript export.

### 6. Writer Presets & Timed Sprints
- **Pantser / Discovery Writer**: Minimalist zen interface with sprint tracker.
- **Plotter / Architect**: Full outliner, character bible, and 3-act story beats.
- **Plantser / Hybrid**: Flexible chapter drawer + organic drafting.
- **Timed Word Sprints**: 10, 15, 20, 30 min timed sprints with celebration confetti.
